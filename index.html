Riskwise Invest

A Windows app for new investors and traders in India: learn the method, practise with paper money, test ideas on past prices and build good habits before putting real money at risk.

See recommended books   The app is in development: downloads coming soon.

What's inside

Academy

Short lessons with charts and tests: reading charts, trading safely, mutual funds, fundamental and technical analysis.

Paper trading

Practise with virtual money, real market prices and real charges and taxes, and keep a trading journal.

Strategy Lab

Test trading ideas on years of past prices, on one stock, your own list or the Nifty indices, before risking money.

Watchlist & research

Track stocks with trend, buy zones, stops and fundamentals explained in plain words.

Mutual funds

Compare funds by cost, consistency and risk, and plan SIPs and goals.

Alerts

Optional Telegram alerts when a stock you follow gets close to its buy zone or a holding hits its stop or target.
